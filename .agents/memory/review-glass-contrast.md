Use an iOS-style translucent glass treatment for review cards while keeping their contents plainly readable in both light and dark themes.

**Why:** The user requested that the glass remain visible across both themes without hiding review content.

**How to apply:** Keep text and controls fully opaque; adapt the card tint, border, and shadow to the active theme, and use restrained backdrop blur.
