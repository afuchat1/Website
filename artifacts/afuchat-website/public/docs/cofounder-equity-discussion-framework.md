# Co-Founder & Equity Discussion Framework

**AfuChat Technologies Limited — discussion checklist**

This document is a preparation aid for discussions. It is not a securities offer, equity grant, promise of ownership, employment agreement or legal advice. No equity is granted by reading this document or submitting an application.

## 1. Role and commitment

- What responsibilities will the person own?
- What decisions can they make, and what requires approval?
- What time commitment and availability are realistic?
- What milestones and outcomes are expected?
- How will progress and disagreements be handled?

## 2. Contribution and resources

- What technical, product, operational or commercial contribution is expected?
- Which tools, infrastructure, funds or other resources will the company provide?
- Who owns existing code, designs, inventions and other pre-existing intellectual property?
- What confidentiality and information-security obligations apply?

## 3. Compensation

- Is any cash compensation currently approved and available?
- If not, how will that be communicated and reviewed?
- Are expenses reimbursable, and who must approve them?
- Do not assume that equity will substitute for a salary unless the parties explicitly discuss and document the arrangement.

## 4. Equity terms to agree separately

If equity is considered, document at least:

- The exact legal entity issuing or transferring the equity.
- The type of equity or ownership interest and the percentage or calculation method.
- The capitalization basis used to calculate any percentage.
- Vesting schedule, cliff, milestones and treatment of any prior contribution.
- Treatment of dilution, future fundraising and changes to the capital structure.
- Voting, information, dividend and other shareholder rights, where applicable.
- Departure, termination, disability, death, transfer and repurchase provisions.
- Intellectual-property assignment, confidentiality and conflict-of-interest terms.
- Required corporate approvals, filings, tax treatment and governing law.

## 5. Governance and decision-making

- Who appoints directors or technical leaders?
- Which decisions require founder or board approval?
- How will deadlocks and disputes be resolved?
- What happens if a founder stops contributing or priorities change?
- How will new founders or senior contributors be evaluated?

## 6. Due diligence before signing

Each party should have a chance to review the company structure, existing obligations, intellectual property, financial position and proposed documents. Obtain independent legal and tax advice appropriate to the jurisdiction before signing or transferring equity.

## Guiding principle

Discuss expectations early and plainly. Never describe an unapproved or undocumented equity possibility as guaranteed ownership. Agree on role, responsibilities, compensation and any equity in signed written documents before relying on those terms.

*Prepared for discussion only. This document does not create contractual rights or obligations.*
