# Developer & Co-Founder Opportunity Guide

**AfuChat Technologies Limited (ATL)**  
**Programme:** Developer and Co-Founder Expression of Interest

## About the opportunity

AfuChat Technologies Limited builds and develops a portfolio of digital products. We welcome thoughtful engineers, designers and product-minded contributors who want to help build, improve and scale products as part of an early-stage venture.

This is not a conventional salaried job offer. A salary is not guaranteed, and submitting an expression of interest does not create an employment relationship, founder status or entitlement to equity.

## Contribution departments

- **Frontend Engineering:** web interfaces, React, Next.js, TypeScript, accessibility and design systems.
- **Backend Engineering:** APIs, Node.js/NestJS, databases, authentication, integrations and service architecture.
- **Mobile Development:** Android, iOS, React Native, Flutter and mobile experiences.
- **AI & Intelligent Search:** applied AI, search, retrieval, ranking and automation.
- **Cloud & DevOps:** infrastructure, CI/CD, deployments, observability, security and reliability.
- **Product Design (UI/UX):** user research, interaction design, prototypes and design systems.
- **Quality & Application Security:** test automation, quality assurance, secure coding and security review.
- **Technical Leadership & Architecture:** architecture decisions, engineering standards, technical planning and mentoring.
- **Product, Data & Growth:** product planning, analytics, documentation, developer experience and adoption.

Department labels describe areas of contribution, not a guarantee that every position is currently open.

## What to include in an introduction

1. Your name and preferred contact email.
2. The department and contribution type that interest you.
3. Relevant skills, technologies and experience.
4. GitHub, portfolio or project links, where available.
5. Your realistic availability and preferred way of collaborating.
6. A short explanation of what you would like to contribute.

Do not send passwords, private keys, identity documents or other sensitive information in an initial application.

## Possible contribution paths

Depending on experience, need and mutual fit, discussions may cover a developer/contributor role, technical lead responsibilities, mentoring, or a potential co-founder role. Titles and responsibilities are not automatic and must be agreed explicitly.

## Compensation and equity

This is an early-stage venture opportunity, not a promise of salary or guaranteed income. Equity may be discussed where appropriate, but is not automatic and is not promised by this guide. Any equity arrangement must be assessed, negotiated and documented separately, including the amount or calculation, vesting, responsibilities, decision rights, intellectual-property terms, departure provisions and applicable legal requirements.

## Selection process

1. Submit an expression of interest.
2. The team reviews your experience and preferred contribution.
3. Discuss product needs, fit, availability and expectations.
4. Agree in writing on the role, responsibilities and applicable terms before work begins.

## Contact

Use the application form on the Join the Team page. It prepares an email to **hello@afuchat.com** for you to review and send. Your details are not transmitted by the website form itself.
